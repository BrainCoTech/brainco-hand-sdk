Revo2 CAN FD raw frame reference

Extended 29-bit CAN IDs; CAN FD with BRS enabled; master ID=1.
Register words: big endian. Modbus CRC16: low byte first.
TX data includes physical CAN FD padding. Padding is excluded from CRC16.
All RX examples below are protocol-derived examples, NOT captured traffic.
EXAMPLE-SN is a placeholder; actual data and checksums depend on the device.
